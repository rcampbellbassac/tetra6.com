About

Tetra6 is an independent consulting and technical practice, built on 20+ years of hands-on experience across IT, systems and network engineering, cybersecurity, and software development.

The work spans a deliberately broad range — infrastructure and security engineering, web and mobile development, data and analytics — applied wherever it's actually needed, from government and mission-driven organizations to independent and community projects. Some of that work is public; see Projects below for real, shipped examples rather than a services list.

  • Systems, network & security engineering
  • Web & mobile software development
  • Mission-oriented and community-driven information systems

Tetra6 currently operates as an independent practice. Formal incorporation (LLC) is planned but not yet complete.
